Work–Energy Theorem

Net work done on an object equals the change in its kinetic energy. Explore how force and displacement shape an object's speed.

Wnet = ΔEk = ½mvf² − ½mvi²

Object & Motion

Mass (m)2 kg
Initial speed (vi)3 m/s
Net Force (Fnet)10 N
Displacement (d)4 m

Theorem Results

Initial KE — ½mvi²9.0 J
Net Work Done (Wnet)40.0 J
